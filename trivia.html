Official Rules & Scoring

Schedule: Each round opens Monday at 9:00 AM and closes Friday at 12:00 PM (noon), Central Time (America/Chicago). Five BMW M history questions are published per round.

Current live scoring: +20 points per correct answer, −10 points per incorrect answer, and +25 bonus points for answering all five correctly. Maximum: 125 points per round. Each enrolled member may submit one completed attempt per round. Answers cannot be changed after submission.

Speed scoring — coming soon: Once the timed system is activated, each question will have a 30-second limit. Correct within 10 seconds: +25; over 10 through 20 seconds: +20; over 20 through 30 seconds: +15; incorrect or unanswered: −10. Five correct answers earn an additional +25 bonus. Maximum: 150 points. The timer and points will be validated by the server. Until activation, the current live scoring above applies.

Enrollment: Sign in and enroll for the month before a round opens to be eligible for that round. New participants are not penalized for rounds that opened before their enrollment. Enrolled eligible members who miss a round may receive missed-question penalties.

Monthly leaderboard: Weekly points accumulate within the Central Time calendar month. Negative totals are possible. Only the top three usernames and scores are displayed publicly. The highest-ranked eligible member may be invited to submit a BMW photo for the Community Spotlight, subject to permission and moderation. Tie-break rules and any prize terms will be published before awards are offered.

A browser cannot reliably prevent looking up answers on another device. The competition uses authenticated accounts, private answer keys, server-side scoring, and one attempt per round to discourage abuse.
