How the challenge works

Five new historical questions open every Monday at 9:00 AM and close the following Tuesday at 12:00 PM (noon), using America/Chicago time. Each correct answer earns 20 points; each wrong or missed answer costs 10 points; answering all five correctly earns a 25-point bonus. Weekly points add up within each calendar month. The highest-ranked eligible player wins that month's Community Spotlight opportunity. Only the top three usernames and scores appear on the public leaderboard. Missed-week penalties apply only to registered players eligible for that round. A verified account, server-side grading, and official contest rules will be required before prizes are offered.
