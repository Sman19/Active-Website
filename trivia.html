Official Rules & Scoring

Schedule: Each round opens Monday at 9:00 AM and closes Friday at 12:00 PM (noon), Central Time (America/Chicago). Five BMW M history questions are published per round.

Timed scoring: Correct within 10 seconds: +25; over 10 through 20 seconds: +20; over 20 through 30 seconds: +15. Wrong or timed-out answers: −10. Five correct answers earn +25 bonus (maximum 150). Answers are locked immediately and times are recorded on the server.

Enrollment: Sign in and enroll for the month before a round opens to be eligible for that round. New participants are not penalized for rounds that opened before their enrollment. Enrolled eligible members who miss a round may receive missed-question penalties.

Monthly leaderboard: Weekly points accumulate within the Central Time calendar month. Negative totals are possible. Only the top three usernames and scores are displayed publicly. After the final Friday round closes, the monthly champion can submit one BMW photo for the Community Spotlight. The winner receives an in-site notification when signed in. Photos require administrator approval before publication. Tie-break rules and any prize terms will be published before awards are offered.

A browser cannot reliably prevent looking up answers on another device. The competition uses authenticated accounts, private answer keys, server-side scoring, and one attempt per round to discourage abuse.
