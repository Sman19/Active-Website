About BMW M Archiv

BMW M Archiv is an independently developed project dedicated to documenting BMW M vehicles and making their factory configurations, model differences, and production history easier to explore.

Enter the member archive

Why the archive exists

BMW M vehicles can vary considerably even within the same model year. Body styles, drivetrains, factory colors, interiors, equipment packages, and individual options all contribute to what makes a particular vehicle distinct.

BMW M Archiv brings those details into a consistent research environment so enthusiasts can study how vehicles were configured and compare differences across model generations. The project is built around careful organization of vehicle information rather than a conventional classified listing or dealership inventory.

Vehicles and generations covered

E9x M3

Research into the V8-generation M3, including E90 sedan and E92 coupe configurations, model-year distinctions, and factory equipment.

E82 1 Series M Coupe

Factory configurations and production-related research for the limited-production 1 Series M Coupe.

F87 M2

Documentation of early F87 M2 vehicles and their model-specific configurations.

G80 M3

Research into the modern M3 sedan, including trim and drivetrain differences, exterior and interior choices, and factory option terminology.

Coverage is developed over time. Availability and completeness of individual records may differ by model and model year.

What members can explore

Within the member archive, the project provides tools for examining vehicle-specific records, searching supported models, reviewing available factory specifications, and comparing configurations. Some models also include equipment and historical MSRP references where available.

Vehicle information is compiled and organized for enthusiast research. Historical pricing and equipment references may vary by market, production date, and source; they should not be treated as a substitute for original manufacturer documentation.

How the information is organized

BMW M Archiv aims to preserve useful distinctions such as model codes, production years, paint and upholstery descriptions, and factory option identifiers. These details help avoid confusing similarly named vehicles or assuming that every car within a generation was built alike.

Records may be refined as additional source information becomes available. The archive is an ongoing independent research project, not an official BMW factory database.

Why membership is required

Detailed vehicle-level research and database tools are available through a member sign-in. Public pages introduce the project and its research focus, while membership helps manage access to the underlying collection and its interactive features.
